# Smell Check overlay

House overlay on `smellcheck`. Core bans live in the package. This file
is only what is true here.

**Package:** `smellcheck`. Canonical files in the sibling repo, or
https://smellcheck.dev/rules/core.md

Do not restate the hard bans. Do not fork `core.md`.

## Pronouns

- Landing / marketing / docs: **you** for the reader. The product
  speaks. No corporate we. No builder I.
- Posts: same, unless the post is signed.

## Terms usually allowed here (not exhaustive)

| Term | Why it earns its keep |
| --- | --- |
| emphasis / stress / reading | The job |
| silent reader | Who supplies the missing pulse |
| report.md / finding | The deliverable |
| teaching sentence | The seven-word example |

## Protected lines

- Did the reader stress the word you meant?
- Earn the word.
- Spray the prose, not the author.
- Have you smell-checked that?

## Genre extras (house law only)

npm, domain, and GitHub belong on Install, not in the hero or About.
Do not inventory the family as "Sibling of X and Y." Name a sibling
when you are saying what this skill is not.
