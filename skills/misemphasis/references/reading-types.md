# Reading types

Classify before you flag. One type per finding. If two types fit,
pick the one that names the contrast the silent reader would miss.

## emphasis_shift

Same words. Stress on a different content word changes who did
what, to whom, or whether it happened.

Teaching case: *I didn't say we should cancel Friday.*

Need a plausible contrast, not a recitation of every word.

## negation_scope

*Not* / *n't* / *never* can attach to the verb, to a later noun,
or to a reason clause. Stress and placement both move the scope.

*I didn't skip the standup because I was busy* — denial of the
skip, or denial of the motive.

## exclusive_particle

*Only*, *even*, *just*, *almost*, *at least*. The particle
associates with the stressed constituent.

*We only promised the beta users a refund* — only promised (no
other commitment), only beta users, or only a refund.

## pronoun_stress

Stress on a pronoun or demonstrative contrasts referents.

*I never said we should invite **him*** — him, not her; or
invite, not assign.

If the pronoun has one obvious antecedent and no contrast is
in the passage, skip it.

## quantifier_scope

*All*, *some*, *any*, *every*. Stress and *not* move how many
the claim covers.

*I didn't invite everyone* — invited none, or invited some.

## Do not invent types

If it is not one of these, it is probably grammar, tone, or
structure. Leave it for another bottle.
