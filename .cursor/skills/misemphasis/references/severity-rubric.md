# Severity

Assign one bucket. A false `likely_misread` costs more than a
missed `judgment_call`.

## likely_misread

A typical silent reader could take a reading the author may not
have meant, and more than one reading is live in ordinary use.

The teaching sentence belongs here. So does an *only* that can
attach to two nearby nouns.

## possible_misread

A second reading is available if someone goes looking. Most
readers will take the default.

Use this when context almost locks it, but a hostile or hurried
reader could still flip the stress.

## judgment_call

Context probably locks the reading. A careful author might still
want a rewrite that names the contrast.

If you cannot name two live readings in a short clause each,
drop the finding instead of stretching this bucket.

## Not a finding

- One obvious reading and no plausible contrast
- The author already marked stress (caps, italics, bold)
- Grammar, tone, AI smell, structural tangles
- Spoken transcript with stress already annotated
