*I didn't say we should cancel Friday.*

Cover one word. The claim moves. That is what silent reading does to every sentence that can take a contrast.

Do not stress every word. Name the other live reading, or leave the sentence alone. Most sentences have one reading. The ones that do not are the ones you are about to ship by accident.
